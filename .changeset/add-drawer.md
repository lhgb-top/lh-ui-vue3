Add the LhDrawer component: an edge-anchored sliding panel with four directions (`right` / `left` / `top` / `bottom`), configurable size, mask click and Escape dismissal, body scroll locking, header / body / footer slots, and dialog accessibility semantics — focus moves into the panel on open, Tab cycles inside, and focus returns to the trigger on close. Shares the new focus-trap and positioning infrastructure with the overlay family.
